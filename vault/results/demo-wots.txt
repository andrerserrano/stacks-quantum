A vault guarded by WOTS+ hash-based signatures
Simnet, Epoch 4.0, Clarity 6. The only cryptography in the signature check is SHA-256.

1. Alice makes a one-time key on her device and opens a vault under its hash.
     vault eb708a434a523b85... under key c0bf766efc7d37cf...
     deposited 100 STX and 0.5 sBTC

2. Mallory has Alice's Stacks account key. He sends withdrawals from her own account.
     with no signature                                          REFUSED: the signature does not match
     with a made-up signature                                   REFUSED: the signature does not match
     signed with a one-time key of his own                      REFUSED: the signature does not match
     The vault never looks at who sent the transaction. Her account key opens nothing.

3. Alice signs a withdrawal of 25 STX to Bob with her one-time key.
     signature: 68 words, 2,176 bytes
   Mallory sees it in the mempool and tries to change it before it confirms.
     recipient changed to Mallory                               REFUSED: the signature does not match
     amount raised to 100 STX                                   REFUSED: the signature does not match
     next key swapped for his own                               REFUSED: the signature does not match
     asset changed, so 25 STX becomes 0.25 sBTC                 REFUSED: the signature does not match

4. Anyone can submit the request as Alice signed it. Here Mallory does.
     unchanged                                                  PAID
     Bob received 25 STX. The vault holds 75 STX.
     cost: 1,208,571 runtime, 0.024% of a tenure's budget
     the vault is now under key 88ef0aa338e3dc46... and the old key is spent

5. Mallory replays the request.
     the same request again                                     REFUSED: the nonce was already used
     the same request, claiming the new nonce and a new key     REFUSED: the signature does not match

6. Alice withdraws sBTC with her next key.
     0.1 sBTC to Bob                                            PAID
     Bob received 0.1 sBTC. The vault holds 75 STX and 0.4 sBTC.

What this shows: the vault pays only what its one-time key signed, once.
What it does not show: the transaction that carries the signature, Stacks blocks
and the Bitcoin behind sBTC are still secured by elliptic-curve signatures.
